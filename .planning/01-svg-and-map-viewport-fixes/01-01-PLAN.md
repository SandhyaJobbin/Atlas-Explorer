**Objective:** Fix the North America SVG viewBox so Canada is fully visible on all screens, and ensure correct aspect ratio rendering across browsers.

<tasks>

<task id="1" type="execute">
<name>Fix SVG viewBox to include Canada</name>
<read_first>
- app/public/maps/north-america.svg
- .planning/01-svg-and-map-viewport-fixes/01-CONTEXT.md
</read_first>
<action>
Open `app/public/maps/north-america.svg`. Change the viewBox attribute on the root `<svg>` element from `-189.42 -50 1157.42 830` to `-189.42 -780 1157.42 1420`. This expands the y-start from -50 to -780 to include Canada's northernmost paths (which extend to y ≈ -771), and increases the y-height from 830 to 1420 to cover the full vertical range.
